Our logo is available in three formats. For most use cases prefer the main logo. Use the plain logo only if you intend to place it against a background that is already orange. For all other background colours use the main logo to offset our logo structure from the surrounding text.

Generally, out logo will be contained within a bounding shape. In the main logo and the monochromatic logo this is a circle. [Variations of our logo](#variations) keep the plain logo intact and alter the bounding shape.

Use the monochromatic logo interchangeably with the main logo but only when the WWC identity has already been established elsewhere in context with the use of the main logo.

## Logo spacing guide

Our rules for designing with our logo are simple because we like to encourage our members to use our branding for buttons, on projects and in other creative ways. We only require that sufficient space is left around the logo to separate it visually from its surroundings. The *minimum space* you should leave is equal to the space between the plain logo and the edge of the bounding shape (shown using white circles in the figure below).

<img src="/identity/wwc-logo-spacing-guide.svg">

In case there is no bounding shape, such as with the plain logo, the *minimum space* is equal to the gap between the base of the pencil tip and the open circle of the logo (shown using a filled cyan circle in the figure above).

Where there is text *associated with the logo* place it to the right of the logo two *minimum spaces* away. Where there is a second bounding box that clarifies a text is associated with the logo – such as on a button – you may place the text wherever you wish, but preferably still to the right of the logo. In any case, prefer ragged-right alignment for all text.

The nearest text not associated with the logo should be placed a minimum of two logo-widths away from the outer edge of the logo (shown using empty cyan circles in the figure above, where any text not associated with the WWC logo will have to be placed outside the figure itself.

Above all, play by eye.

# Variations

<img src="/identity/WWC-Forum-favicon-PNG.png" class="w-full md:w-1/4 md:float-right md:ml-3 md:mb-1">

The WWC logo is designed to adapt to variations. A typical example of this is our use of the logo as an easily identifiable favicon differentiating the main website from the forum (pictured) through a different bounding shape. In such cases, the bounding shape should have a black outline and should be filled in its entirety with one of the WWC brand colours, preferrably orange. The sizes of the inset logo and the bounding shape (including the outline) should be such that their relative sizes are preserved when overlaid with the main logo.
